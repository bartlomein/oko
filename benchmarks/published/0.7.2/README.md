# Published benchmark results for Oko 0.7.2

Pi 1.0.0 with GPT-5.6 Sol, low thinking: nine branch-suite tasks, three
repetitions, native versus Oko with setup guidance and prefetch (54 sessions).
Measured on 2 October 2026 before the version-only release bump.

`agent-sessions/report-pi.json` preserves all per-session metrics and grades.
`agent-sessions/summary.json` contains aggregates. Raw transcripts, credentials,
source snapshots and local paths are excluded.

See [methodology and limitations](../../../docs/benchmark-results.md#pi-54-sessions-october-2026).
Other clients and retrieval benchmarks were not rerun for this release; their
previous results remain under [0.7.0](../0.7.0/).
